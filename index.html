The Street

A Street That Earns Its Reputation

Bree Street didn't happen overnight. Over a decade, it transformed from a nondescript CBD throughway into Cape Town's most dynamic culinary and creative address — driven by bold restaurateurs, independent galleries and a CCID-backed precinct management that keeps it safe, clean and open for business.

Today it's the address where Liam Tomlin's Chefs Warehouse anchors a block that also holds Nikkei's Japanese-Peruvian fusion, Youngblood Africa's gallery and Kluk CGDT's couture atelier. That's Bree — cosmopolitan, creative, and unapologetically Cape Town.

CCID Precinct

Safe, Clean & Open for Business

Bree Street sits within the Cape Town Central City Improvement District — a 1.6km² managed precinct with 24-hour security patrols, CCTV coverage and rapid incident response. The CCID has transformed the CBD into one of Africa's most investable urban environments.
